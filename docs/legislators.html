Research notes

Methods and limitations

Directional balance, not an ideology test. Color combines reviewed legislative evidence with separately labeled candidate-supplied Vote Smart questionnaire dimensions.

Vote Smart is sparse. Five of the 30 focal candidates have a scored questionnaire available at or before the focal election. The 1994 archive has no scored questionnaire profiles. Missing responses are never coded as moderate or replaced with group ratings.

Public positions remain legible. Campaign statements appear even when their wording has not been normalized into a directional score.

Timing matters. The latest questionnaire no later than the focal election may be shown, with its year preserved. Later answers never leak backward.

This project uses LegiScan, ALISON, Vote Smart candidate-supplied Political Courage Tests, campaign materials, contemporary reporting, reviewed bill text, and the Candidate Margin Overperformance model. Read the CMO methodology.
