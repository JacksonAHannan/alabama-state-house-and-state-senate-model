The groupings

Five position profiles, formed without looking at any election result

Clustering uses the eight ontology issue families only. Residual WAR, wins and incumbency are attached afterwards, so no electoral outcome can place a member in a group. Unscored candidate-cycles are seats with no contested general election; they are not zeros.

      Overperformance against the reference model

      WAR is the candidate-oriented race residual in two-party margin points: the actual legislative-minus-ticket gap minus the fitted structural expectation of the fixed 2018–24 reference model. Pre-2016 cycles are scored against modern partisan expectations, so early-era Democrats show large positive WAR partly by construction.

        How much to trust this

        Cluster sensitivity, stated plainly

        Each bar is an adjusted Rand index between the published grouping and the grouping produced by an alternative reasonable choice: 1.0 is identical, 0 is no better than chance. These are descriptive tendencies, not formal caucus membership.

        What these groups are not

        They are not the Alabama Legislative Black Caucus or any other organised caucus, which have real membership rolls this analysis does not use. They are not a pre-election snapshot: a member’s later roll calls help define the group used to read their earlier race. They are not causal — a group’s mean WAR describes the races its members happened to run, including opponent quality and local conditions this measure cannot separate.

        How this page was produced

        Grouping

        WAR definition

        The residual framing follows Split Ticket’s WAR methodology; this Alabama implementation and its estimates are independent. Race WAR = actual legislative-minus-ticket gap minus the fitted structural expected gap. Democratic WAR is the race residual and Republican WAR is its exact negative. One fixed reference model, fitted on 2018–24 Southern races, scores every cycle; no era-specific refit is applied. Career cumulative WAR is the sum of a person’s scored cycle WAR. No pooled individual effect, fundraising term, or ideology term enters WAR.
