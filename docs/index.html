How to read this forecast

Basic is the default guardrail: the poll-adjusted presidential baseline plus 20% of CMO expected performance.

Fundamentals+ uses the same inputs and applies the full CMO expected-performance adjustment. It is more responsive to district context and less conservative than Basic.

Conditional probability estimates.

District probabilities use a zero-centered six-point normal calibration, selected after forward-cycle and leave-state-out tests on 1,188 recent Southern legislative races. Basic and Fundamentals+ use the same calibration applied to their respective margins.

Seat distributions are exact independent-seat calculations conditional on the displayed national environment. A different national environment should be viewed as a separate shared scenario, not hidden inside every district's error band.

Districts with only one major-party nominee are treated as fixed Democratic or Republican seats in chamber summaries. Independent-only and otherwise unresolved districts remain unmodeled and are displayed in gray.

The national-environment projection uses polling through the date shown above and should be refreshed as new polling becomes available.
