## Authority decision (Alabama 2018 and 2022 legislative contest totals)

The certified State Canvassing Board canvass is the authoritative source for Alabama
2018 and 2022 general-election legislative contest totals. The Alabama canonical
candidate route stays the outcome source family (preserving stable candidate IDs,
names and finance identity links), but each canonical candidate is bridged to exactly
one certified canvass cell at exact year/chamber/district/party grain with name
evidence. Canonical totals that disagree with the bridged certified cell are corrected
to the certified value under a registered repair with before-images; the bridge row
records the correction. The Southern outcome mart derives its scalar `source_file_id`
and its third-party vote total from the bridged certified observation set and refuses
to build when a bridged canonical total disagrees with the canvass.

Evidence at decision time: 2018 has 204 canonical D/R rows, 193 equal to certified and
11 lower (all equal to the incomplete precinct subtotal); 2022 has 173 rows, 163 equal
and 10 lower. Every canonical row matches one certified cell; no unmatched or ambiguous
rows. Corrected strict Southern races: 2018 HD81, HD83, SD14, SD27; 2022 HD32, HD68,
HD92. No winner flag changes.
