# Headline ideology tournament

Nine headline dimensions replace the flat issue menu. Gun rights and punitive law-and-order are intentionally separate. Tax incidence remains a drill-down and is not averaged into a single tax score.

A score is the mean of available oriented components. This maximizes coverage but `components_observed` must remain visible because a one-component score is less comprehensive than a multi-component score.

## Definitions

| headline_dimension | positive_pole | negative_pole | components |
|---|---|---|---|
| social_traditionalism | traditional Christian and abortion-restrictive | social and reproductive autonomy | christian_sexual_morality, abortion_access |
| racial_and_political_equality | expand racial and voting equality | restrict or preserve status quo | racial_civil_rights, voting_access |
| gun_rights | expand gun rights | strengthen gun restrictions | gun_access, gun_purchase_regulation |
| punitive_law_and_order | punitive enforcement | rehabilitation and due process | criminal_punishment, incarceration, police_authority, due_process, drug_criminalization |
| labor_power | expand labor power | favor management and restrict labor | labor_rights, labor_capital_alignment, public_employee_compensation |
| material_redistribution | expand unconditional material support | restrict or condition support | welfare_generosity, welfare_conditionality |
| public_services | expand public services | reduce public provision | education_public_funding, healthcare_access, childcare_support, education_accountability |
| market_and_development_autonomy | market, choice, and development autonomy | government direction and preservation | market_governance, education_market_choice, resource_development, environmental_protection, renewable_energy_support |
| institutional_democratic_reform | expand popular control and campaign regulation | institutional control and fewer restrictions | campaign_finance_restrictions, institutional_populism |
