Alabama WAR is the race-level residual from the post-2016 Southern structural model. For every contested Democratic-versus-Republican Alabama general election after 2016:

`WAR = actual legislative-minus-federal gap - fitted structural expected gap`

The Democratic candidate receives the race residual and the Republican receives its negative. There is no candidate pooling, career average, or fundraising adjustment in WAR. The same-cycle fit is a retrospective rating; cross-cycle forecast errors are separate.

Coverage is 97 races and 194 candidate-cycle rows across 2018 and 2022.
