# Pre-1998 Alabama legislative-journal holdings

Section 63 of the Alabama Constitution requires final passage to be recorded by
yeas and nays with member names entered in the journal. Consequently, a missing
digital roll call must be treated as a source or extraction gap until the actual
journal has been inspected.

## What is local

- The locally downloaded `alabama_legislature/acts` volumes for 1986–1998 are
  session laws, not House or Senate journals. They cannot supply member votes.
- The ADAH electronic House collection begins in 1998.
- The ADAH electronic Senate collection contains three 1995 organizational-day
  PDFs, then daily journals beginning in 1998. Its 2000 folder contains indexes
  and a roster but no daily journals.

## What exists outside the electronic collection

ADAH finding aid GR-LEG-42 identifies physical House-journal containers for the
period, including SG016611–SG016621 (1988), SG016573–SG016582 (1989),
SG017041–SG017049 (1990), SG019804–SG019814 (1991), SG020854–SG020865
(1992), SG021489–SG021499 (1993), SG021478–SG021486 (1994),
SG023576–SG023585 (1995), SG023837–SG023845 (1996), and
SG018203–SG018218 (1997). The Alabama Supreme Court and State Law Library also
reports House and Senate journals from 1819 to the present in its holdings.

These records strongly imply that the 1988–1997 final-passage name lists exist,
but the relevant volumes have not yet been located as downloadable scans.
Digitization or a reproduction request is required before parsing them.
