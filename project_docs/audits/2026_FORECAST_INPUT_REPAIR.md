# 2026 forecast input repair

## Result

The post-2016 polling-CMO experiment and promoted headline artifacts were
rebuilt from the corrected 2026 finance and incumbency inputs. The model
specification and probability calibration were not changed.

## SD-7 case audit

| Component | Prior published input/output | Corrected rebuild |
|---|---:|---:|
| Democratic incumbent | No | No |
| Republican incumbent | No | Yes |
| Jared Sluss fundraising | $0.00 | $19,146.16 |
| Sam Givhan fundraising | $268,708.37 | $370,509.84 |
| Incumbency adjustment, Democratic margin | 0.00 | -8.70 |
| Fundraising adjustment, Democratic margin | -5.05 | +0.92 |
| Headline Democratic margin | -5.15 | -7.87 |
| Democratic win probability | 20.6% | 11.5% |

The positive corrected fundraising residual does not mean the Democrat raised
more money. The headline model first estimates the finance gap expected from
district structure and incumbency and then uses the within-cycle residual. The
observed Republican advantage is slightly smaller than that first stage
expects. The much larger corrected incumbency term leaves the race clearly
Republican-favored.

The transient SD-7 movement that prompted this repair was caused by two input
errors operating together: Sam Givhan was incorrectly marked as a
nonincumbent, and his 2025 finance total was being replaced rather than added
to 2026 activity.

## Whole-output checks

- 48 headline races were produced, with one row per chamber and district.

- No race changed its predicted winner relative to the prior published
  artifact.

  movements in races whose sitting senators had previously been missed. Those
