SYNOPSIS:          Under existing law, the State8
Superintendent of Education is authorized to revoke9
the teaching or other educational certificate of10
any person who is guilty of immoral conduct or11
unbecoming or indecent behavior.12
Also, under existing law, the employment13
contract, or employment, of an education employee14
may be cancelled or terminated for incompetency,15
neglect of duty, immorality, and other good and16
just cause.17
This bill would require the State18
Superintendent of Education to revoke the teaching19
or other educational certificate of any person20
convicted of a Class A felony or sex offense21
involving a child and would provide for the22
immediate cancellation of the employment contract23
or employment of any education employee convicted24
of a felony or sex offense involving a child.25
