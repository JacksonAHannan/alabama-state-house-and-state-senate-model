8 RECOGNIZING RED BAY HOSPITAL FOR 50 YEARS OF SERVICE
9 TO THE COMMUNITY.
10  
11 WHEREAS, highest commendations and congratulations
12 are herein proudly presented to Red Bay Hospital on the
13 occasion of its 50th year serving the Red Bay community and
14 surrounding areas; and
15 WHEREAS, since its inception in 1967, Red Bay
16 Hospital has been an integral part of the well-being of the
17 people of Northwest Alabama and Northeast Mississippi; and
18 WHEREAS, in 2005, Red Bay Hospital was awarded the
19 status of Critical Access Hospital by the State of Alabama and
20 the Center for Medicare and Medicaid Services; and 
21 WHEREAS, Red Bay Hospital is one of only four
22 Critical Access hospitals in the State of Alabama, which means
23 that the citizens the hospital serves can count on access to
24 quality health care within a reasonable distance from their
25 homes; and
Page 1

1 WHEREAS, Red Bay Hospital is a non-profit hospital
2 owned and operated by the Colbert County-Northwest Alabama
3 Health Care Authority; and 
4 WHEREAS, Red Bay Hospital, along with its dedicated
5 staff, is committed to providing cost-effective care with
6 access for all who are in need; and
7 WHEREAS, Red Bay Hospital is proud to display its
8 new logo, "50 Years of Healing, 50 Years of Caring"; now
9 therefore,
10 BE IT RESOLVED BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF
11 THE LEGISLATURE OF ALABAMA, That Red Bay Hospital is highly
12 honored and commended, and this resolution is offered in
13 deepest admiration and appreciation, along with best wishes
14 for all future endeavors.
