SYNOPSIS:          Under existing law, juvenile probation8
officers in counties having a population of 99,0009
or less according to the 1990 federal census are10
state employees under the administration of the11
Administrative Office of Courts, and juvenile12
probation officers in counties having a population13
of more than 99,000 are county employees and paid14
on a pay scale set by the county but subsidized by15
the state.16
This bill would provide that if the17
Administrative Office of Courts reduces funding by18
a certain percentage for state juvenile probation19
services, the salary subsidies for those juvenile20
probation officers in counties having a population21
of more than 99,000 according to the 1990 federal22
decennial census shall be reduced by the same23
percentage.24
