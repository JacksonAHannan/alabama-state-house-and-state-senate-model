SYNOPSIS:          Under existing law, the execution of a8
death sentence is performed by the warden of9
William C. Holman Prison, or his or her deputy, by10
electrocution at Atmore, Alabama.11
This bill would place a moratorium on the12
death penalty for a period of three years until13
certain procedures are implemented to ensure that14
death penalty cases are administered fairly and15
impartially.16

Section 1. (a) There shall be a moratorium of three1

Code of Alabama 1975.4
(b) During the moratorium provided in subsection5
(a), the following procedures shall be implemented to ensure6

(1) The American Bar Association Guidelines for the10

(2) Due process procedures to preserve and enhance12
state post conviction relief in death sentence cases.13
(3) Procedures to eliminate discrimination in14
capital sentencing on the basis of race of either the victim15
or defendant.16
(4) Due process procedures to prevent the execution17
of mentally retarded persons and persons who were under the18
age of 18 at the time of the offense.19
Section 2. This act shall become effective on the20
