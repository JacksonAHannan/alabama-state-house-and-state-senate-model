13 Relating to Lauderdale County; to amend Section 2 of
14 Act 791, 1969 Regular Session (Acts 1969, p. 1421), as last
15 amended by Act 79-683, 1979 Regular Session (Acts 1979, p.
16 1213); to further provide for the uniform allowance for
17 officers and employees in the sheriff's office; and to provide
18 that this act shall be retroactive to October 1, 2006.
19 BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF ALABAMA:
20 Section 1. Section 2 of Act 791, 1969 Regular
21 Session (Acts 1969, p. 1421), as last amended by Act 79-683,
22 1979 Regular Session (Acts 1979, p. 1213), is amended to read
23 as follows:
24 "Section 2. (a) The Lauderdale County Community
25 Development Commission shall furnish each deputy sheriff, the
26 chief deputy, each corrections officer, and any other employee
Page 1

1 designated by the sheriff with uniforms and an annual
2 allowance sufficient to maintain the uniforms.
3 (b) Notwithstanding the provisions of Act 2008-125,
4 2008 Regular Session (Acts 2008, p. 166), the Lauderdale
5 County Community Development Commission shall pay for the
6 costs associated with this act from the Community Development
7 Fund created by Act 2008-125 which is comprised of the
8 additional three percent of TVA in-lieu-of-tax payments
9 received pursuant to Section 40-28-2 of the Code of Alabama
10 1975, beginning with the fiscal year which began October 1,
11 2006.
12 Section 2. All laws or parts of laws which conflict
13 with this act are repealed.
14 Section 3. This act shall become effective on the
15 first day of the third month following its passage and
16 approval by the Governor, or its otherwise becoming law.
