SYNOPSIS:         Under existing law, law enforcement officers8
and certain persons issued a license to carry a9
concealed weapon are permitted to carry a deadly10
weapon, or firearm, on K-12 school property. 11
This bill would authorize a local board of12
education to designate employees as school security13
personnel and would allow such persons designated14
as school security personnel, with certain15
qualifications, to carry firearms while on duty.16
This bill would provide that the State17
Department of Education must pay any costs18
associated with training requirements.19
