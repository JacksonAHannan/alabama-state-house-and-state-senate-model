8 SYNOPSIS:         This bill would propose an amendment to the
9 Constitution of Alabama of 1901, to provide for a
10 five year job creation and retention program to be
11 funded with oil and gas capital payments received
12 by the state for the right to explore and drill for
13 or to produce oil, gas or other hydrocarbon
14 minerals in any offshore area of the state.

1 Section 1. The following amendment to the
2 Constitution of Alabama of 1901, as amended, is proposed and
3 shall become valid as a part thereof when approved by a
4 majority of the qualified electors voting thereon and in
5 accordance with Sections 284, 285, and 287 of the Constitution
6 of Alabama of 1901, as amended:
7 PROPOSED AMENDMENT
8 (a) Notwithstanding any other provision of law to
9 the contrary, beginning in the fiscal year ending September
10 30, 2013 and continuing through the fiscal year ending
11 September 30, 2017, all oil and gas capital payments received
12 by the state for the right to explore and drill for or to
13 produce oil, gas, or other hydrocarbon minerals in any
14 offshore area of the state other than the amounts necessary to
15 meet debt service requirement on outstanding general
16 obligation bonds, shall be distributed in equal proportions to
17 each congressional district in the state as these districts
18 exist on the effective date of this amendment and used
19 exclusively to help create jobs and retain existing jobs. For
20 the purposes of this amendment, the term "oil and gas capital
21 payment" shall have the same meaning as provided under
22 Amendment 666 to the Constitution of Alabama of 1901, now
23 appearing as Section 219.04 of the Official Recompilation of
24 the Constitution of 1901, as amended.
25 (b) The Governor in conjunction with the Alabama
26 Development Office, shall decide how the funds provided to
27 each congressional district will be utilized to create and/or

1 retain jobs within each district. Provided, however, none of
2 the funds may be utilized to provided highway infrastructure.
