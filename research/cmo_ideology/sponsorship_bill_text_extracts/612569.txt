2 ENROLLED, An Act,
3 Relating to Franklin County; to propose an amendment
4 to the Constitution of Alabama of 1901, to authorize the
5 governing body of Franklin County to allocate one-fourth of
6 the proceeds from the one cent tax levied in Franklin County
7 for the purpose of matching funds for any grant from the
8 Alabama Transportation Rehabilitation and Improvement Program.
9 BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF ALABAMA:
10 Section 1. The following amendment to the
11 Constitution of Alabama of 1901, is proposed and shall become
12 valid as a part of the Constitution when all requirements of
13 this act are fulfilled:
14 PROPOSED AMENDMENT
15 Notwithstanding the provisions of Section 40-12-4,
16 Code of Alabama 1975, commencing October 1, 2014, the
17 governing body of Franklin County, by ordinance, may allocate
18 one-fourth of the proceeds of the one cent tax levied in
19 Franklin County pursuant to Section 40-12-4, Code of Alabama
20 1975, for the purpose of matching funds for any grant in
21 Franklin County from the Alabama Transportation Rehabilitation
22 and Improvement Program. The authority granted by this
23 amendment is effective for two years. The authority may be
24 extended from time to time for subsequent two-year periods if
