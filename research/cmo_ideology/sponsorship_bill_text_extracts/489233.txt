8 SYNOPSIS:         Under existing law, lotteries and gift
9 enterprises are prohibited.
10 This bill would propose an amendment to
11 Section 65 of the Constitution of Alabama of 1901,
12 to establish an Alabama Lottery to fund certain
13 Education Trust Fund programs, to create an Alabama
14 Lottery Corporation to implement and to regulate
15 the operation of such lottery, and to prohibit the
16 operation of casinos in the State.
17 The amendment dedicates the net proceeds
18 from the lottery to the Education Trust Fund for
19 classroom instructional supplies, resource officers
20 and scholarships. The amendment provides that
21 lottery proceeds will not supplant or replace
22 existing education revenues, establishes the
23 Lottery Trust Fund, and authorizes the Legislature
24 to pass general laws to implement the amendment.

11 "(a) Establishment of an Alabama Lottery.
12 "The Legislature shall, by general law, provide for

14 of an Alabama Lottery.

16 Prohibited.
17 "Funds deposited into the Education Trust Fund which
18 are derived from the Alabama Lottery shall not replace or
19 supplant existing or any other funds dedicated or intended for
20 the purposes of such Fund.
21 "(c) Lottery Trust Fund.
22 "The proceeds from the Alabama Lottery shall be
23 applied first to the payment of the expenses of administering
24 and operating the Alabama Lottery, including, without
25 limitation, the payment of all prizes, without any further
26 appropriation required by law, and the balance of such
27 proceeds remaining after such payments shall be deposited, as

1 provided by general law, and held in a special, separate
2 account in the State Treasury hereby created and designated
3 the "Lottery Trust Fund" until such time as such proceeds are
4 disbursed pursuant to this amendment.
5 "(d) Alabama Lottery Corporation.
6 "The administration of the Alabama Lottery shall be
7 vested in a corporation hereby created and designated the
8 "Alabama Lottery Corporation." The duties, powers, authority,
9 and composition of the Alabama Lottery Corporation shall be
10 provided by general law.
11 "(e) Allocations and Disbursements.
12 "(1) Monies in the Lottery Trust Fund shall be
13 allocated and disbursed to the Education Trust Fund to be used
14 exclusively (a) first, to increase the annual appropriation
15 for classroom instructional supplies to $1,000 per teacher
16 unit; (b) a minimum of $25 million annually to help local
17 public school systems hire, train and equip resource officers
18 to place in the local schools; and (c) the remainder to
19 provide scholarships to citizens of Alabama who are A/B Honor
20 Roll students, as established by the Legislature, to attend
21 universities, colleges, and junior, technical, or community
22 colleges located in Alabama.
23 "(f) Agreements with other Jurisdictions.
24 "The Alabama Lottery Corporation may enter into
25 reciprocal agreements with other jurisdictions for the
26 operation and promotion of games which are permitted to be
27 operated, utilized, or conducted by the Alabama Lottery.
