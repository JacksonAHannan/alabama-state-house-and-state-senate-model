SYNOPSIS:          Under existing law, a public school teacher8
is deemed offered reemployment for the succeeding9
school year at the same salary unless the employing10
board of education provides notice in writing to11
the contrary to the teacher on or before the last12
day of the preceding school term.13
This bill would clarify that the notice14
requirement also applies to a reduction in15
compensation pursuant to the loss of a supplemental16
position.17
