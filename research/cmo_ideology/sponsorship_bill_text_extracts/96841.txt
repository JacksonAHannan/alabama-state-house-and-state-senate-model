SYNOPSIS:          Existing law makes it a crime to knowingly,8
intentionally, or recklessly expose a child to a9
controlled substance, chemical substance, or drug10
paraphernalia.11
This bill would clarify the term "child" to12
include an unborn child in utero at any stage of13
development regardless of viability. This bill14
would establish venue for prosecution for exposure15
in utero in the county where the child is born.16
This bill would create an evidentiary rebuttable17
presumption relating to exposure in utero if both18
the mother and the child test positive for the same19
controlled substance not prescribed by a physician.20
