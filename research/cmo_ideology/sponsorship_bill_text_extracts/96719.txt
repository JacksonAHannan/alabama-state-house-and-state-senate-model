SYNOPSIS:          Under existing law, the "Protection From8
Abuse Act" provides procedures for the issuance of9
protection orders related to domestic violence.10
This bill would revise the "Protection From11
Abuse Act." Among other things, the bill would12
define the crimes subject to protection, further13
provide for the plaintiffs, including persons in a14
dating relationship, who may seek protection15
orders, include threats as subject to protection,16
further provide for the types of cases and17
jurisdiction of the courts to issue protection18
orders, clarify provisions related to uniform acts19
regarding custody and support, provide for the form20
of petitions, increase the time for final hearings,21
provide for the issuance of orders and forms of22
relief, provide for a Protection Order Registry at23
the Administrative Office of Courts, and repeal the24
provisions for criminal penalties from this civil25
statute.26
