SYNOPSIS:          This bill proposes an amendment to the8
Alabama Constitution of 1901 that will limit gaming9
in Alabama, establish the Alabama Gaming Commission10
to regulate commercial bingo operations, assure11
that the proceeds from these operations are12
legitimately accounted for in an open manner, and13
provide for a tax on bingo operators and bingo14
vendors to support local governments and to fund15
the General Fund for the benefit of the state16
Medicaid program and the Education Trust Fund for17
the benefit of the public schools.18

Section 1. Declarations.14
Because Alabama has experienced a proliferation of15
bingo gaming throughout the state, it is of paramount16
importance for such bingo operations to be taxed, limited, and17
regulated. The taxes imposed upon bingo operations and bingo18
vendors in this amendment raise additional revenues for the19
local municipalities and counties where the bingo operations20
are located and fund the state General Fund for the benefit of21
the state Medicaid program and the Education Trust Fund for22
the benefit of the public schools. To prohibit the23
proliferation of illegal and unregulated gaming in the state,24
this amendment limits Bingo to eight (8) points of destination25
in the State of Alabama, which must meet a minimum investment26
requirement in non-gaming amenities to assure that Bingo27

revenues are serving as a catalyst to create jobs and develop1
an entertainment and tourism industry. This amendment further2
creates the Alabama Gaming Commission for the regulation of3
Bingo in the state.4
