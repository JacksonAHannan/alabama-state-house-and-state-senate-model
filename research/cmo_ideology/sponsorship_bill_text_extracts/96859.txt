SYNOPSIS:          Under existing law, a municipality may8
license junkyards that are not regulated by the9
state within its police jurisdiction to the same10
extent as within its corporate limits.11
This bill would require a municipality to12
conduct a public forum and adopt a resolution13
before licensing a junkyard in the police14
jurisdiction and would require approval by the15
county and the municipality.16
The county would regulate any junkyard17

municipality after licensure.19
