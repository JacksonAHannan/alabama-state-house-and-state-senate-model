2 ENROLLED, House Joint Resolution,
3 RECOGNIZING THE ALABAMA STUDENT ANGLER BASS FISHING
4 ASSOCIATION (ASABFA) AS THE OFFICIAL ORGANIZATION FOR STUDENT
5 BASS FISHING ANGLERS OF THE STATE OF ALABAMA.
6  
7 WHEREAS, thousands of Alabama students throughout
8 the state enjoy and participate regularly in fishing; and 
9 WHEREAS, competitive student angler sport fishing is
10 currently developing all over the nation on a formalized basis
11 with the state of Illinois leading the way with over 300 of
12 the state's 785 schools associated with student angler chapter
13 clubs involved in the sport of fishing; and
14 WHEREAS, professional fishing and outdoor
15 organizations such as TBF-FLW Outdoors, Bassmaster, and other
16 organizations have expressed their support for the growth of
17 student angler fishing for students in grades 7-12 as well as
18 at the college level; and 
19 WHEREAS, recognizing organized student angler sport
20 fishing would allow thousands of youths, particularly those
21 not interested in traditional sports, the opportunity to
22 compete individually and on chapter club teams in outdoor
23 competitive fishing events and activities; and 
24 WHEREAS, recognizing organized student angler sport
25 fishing for grades 7-12 in Alabama would increase intrastate

1 tourism and contribute greatly to the economic impact of the
2 communities and counties where scheduled ASABFA sanctioned
3 events and activities are held; and 
4 WHEREAS, a joint resolution passed in both Houses of
5 the Legislature and signed by the Governor, Act 2010-636,
6 encouraged the development of organized student angler fishing
7 in the State of Alabama; and 
8 WHEREAS, no Alabama or national athletic association
9 or agency recognizes, sanctions, or authorizes the formation
10 of student angler chapter clubs that would provide an
11 opportunity for student anglers of our state in grades 7-12 to
12 participate in the competitive sport or activity of fishing;
13 and 
14 WHEREAS, the ASABFA, formed under the Alabama
15 Non-Profit Unincorporated Association Act, is the grassroots,
16 nonprofit tax exempt umbrella organization leading the way for
17 the development of angler fishing chapter clubs that combine
18 the sport of fishing with the thrills of the outdoors on our
19 great Alabama rivers and lakes; and
20 WHEREAS, the ASABFA is an all volunteer organization
21 staffed with extremely experienced anglers to demonstrate the
22 necessary skills and professional experience to student
23 anglers, position them for future leadership roles through the
24 friendly spirit of competition, promote physical exercise, and
25 encourage student anglers to protect and promote the

1 conservation and preservation of the natural resources of our
2 great state; and 
3 WHEREAS, the ASABFA is an organization funded by
4 membership dues, tournament fees, sponsorships, grants, and
5 other contributions; all funds received by the association are
6 expended for operation of the association for the benefit of
7 student anglers; and 
8 WHEREAS, ASABFA does not require authorization for
9 any new expenditure or appropriation of state or local funds
10 by the Legislature; and
11 WHEREAS, the ASABFA Board of Directors will include
12 one director representing the House of Representatives, one
13 director representing the Senate, and one director
14 representing the Governor as oversight for the organization;
15 now therefore,
16 BE IT RESOLVED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF ALABAMA, BOTH
17 HOUSES THEREOF CONCURRING, That we hereby recognize, by copy
18 of this resolution, the Alabama Student Angler Bass Fishing
19 Association as the official organization for student bass
20 fishing anglers in the State of Alabama for grades 7-12.
