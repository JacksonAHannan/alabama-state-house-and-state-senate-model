2 ENROLLED, An Act,
3 Relating to school safety in Etowah County; to allow
4 retired law enforcement officers to serve on a part-time basis
5 as school resource officers in schools in Etowah County.
6 BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF ALABAMA:
7 Section 1. This act shall apply only in Etowah
8 County.
9 Section 2. (a) A person who retired from the
10 position of municipal police officer, deputy sheriff, or other
11 position that required certification by the Alabama Peace
12 Officers' Standards and Training Commission (the commission)
13 and who retired in good standing may be employed as a
14 part-time school resource officer by Etowah County or by a
15 municipality or local school district in Etowah County in
16 accordance with this section.
17 (b) As a part-time employee, a school resource
18 officer shall not be eligible for health insurance or
19 retirement benefits beyond those benefits the person is
20 already receiving through his or her former full-time
21 employment. Even though part-time, a part-time school resource
22 officer may be required to work a schedule that coincides with
23 a full school day schedule for the period when school is in
24 regular operation and as otherwise needed.
Page 1

HB600
1 (c) Part-time school resource officers shall have
2 all rights, privileges, and powers of a law enforcement
3 officer certified by the commission under the laws of the
4 state.
5 (d) A chief of police or sheriff and members of his
6 or her force, as applicable, shall have immunity from civil
7 liability for actions arising from his or her position as a
8 part-time school resource officer.
9 (e) In a municipality that has its own police force,
10 preference in hiring a part-time school resource officer shall
11 be given to a retired municipal police officer. In all other
12 unincorporated areas of Etowah County, preference in hiring
13 shall be given to a retired deputy sheriff.
14 (f) Funding for part-time school resource officers
15 may come from local, state, or federal budgets or from any
16 board of education, or a combination thereof. The rate of pay
17 for a part-time school resource officer shall not be tied to
18 any existing pay scale used by the hiring authority but shall
19 instead be at the discretion of the hiring authority.
20 (g) This section shall not apply to a full-time law
21 enforcement officer serving in the capacity as a school
22 resource officer on a regular basis as of the effective date
23 of this act and employed by a law enforcement agency. A
24 full-time law enforcement officer working in this capacity may

3 Section 3. This act shall become effective on the
4 first day of the third month following its passage and
5 approval by the Governor, or its otherwise becoming law.
