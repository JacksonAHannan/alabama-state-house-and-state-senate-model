SYNOPSIS:          This bill would propose an income tax8
deduction for businesses that hire a person9
presently drawing unemployment compensation or10
whose unemployment compensation benefits have11
expired. Such businesses will receive an income tax12
deduction equal to 50 percent of the gross wages13
paid to each such person hired and retained as an14
employee for 12 consecutive months.  15
