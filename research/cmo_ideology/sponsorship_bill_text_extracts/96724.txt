SYNOPSIS:          Under existing common law, for the purpose8
of criminal homicide, the death of the victim must9
occur within a year and a day after the act causing10
the death.11
This bill would provide that it would not be12
a bar to prosecution for criminal homicide if the13
acts constituting the offense occurred more than a14
year and a day before the death.15
