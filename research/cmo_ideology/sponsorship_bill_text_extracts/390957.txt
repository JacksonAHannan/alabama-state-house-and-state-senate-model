8 SYNOPSIS:         Pursuant to Act 2010-725, the Prepaid
9 Affordable College Tuition (PACT) Program was
10 extensively amended to ensure financial stability
11 for the program. A new separate PACT Board was
12 created and appropriations from the Education Trust
13 Fund were made to the program for fiscal years
14 ending September 30, 2015, and, if necessary,
15 extending through fiscal year ending 2027.
16 Existing law limits the cost of tuition for
17 certain PACT plan contracts.
18 Also under existing law, the various boards
19 of trustees of the public four-year institutions of
20 higher education and the State Department of
21 Postsecondary Education, under the governance of
22 the State Board of Education, for the public
23 two-year institutions of higher education in the
24 state establish the total cost of tuition and fees
25 for their respective institutions.
Page 1

1 This bill would repeal the limit on the cost
2 of tuition for certain PACT plan contract
3 participants.
4 This bill would define the term tuition and
5 mandatory fees for the PACT program, to be the same
6 rate of the current semester the student is
7 enrolled, thereby tying PACT program payments to
8 fluctuation in tuition and fees expenses.
9 This bill would extend tuition caps to all
10 colleges and universities in the state.
11 This bill would require all public
12 institutions to limit annual increases in fees to
13 the average percentage amount such fees were
14 increased over the past 10 years.
15 This bill would restore the state's
16 commitment to pay all benefits promised to program
17 participants. PACT investments would be limited to
18 no more than 60 percent equity investments.
