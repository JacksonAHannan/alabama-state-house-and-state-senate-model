8 MOURNING THE DEATH OF BELLE FARMER.
9  
10 WHEREAS, it is with a deep sense of sadness and
11 regret that we note the death of Belle Farmer of Attalla,
12 Alabama, on April 13, 2011, at the age of 100 years; and
13 WHEREAS, Mrs. Farmer was a member of the Reed
14 Memorial Baptist Church and served as the supervisor of the
15 lunchroom at Stowers Hill Elementary School for 28 years; and
16 WHEREAS, Mrs. Farmer is preceded in death by her
17 husband, Dock Farmer, and son-in-law, William "Bill" Hicks;
18 and
19 WHEREAS, left to cherish her memory are her
20 daughters, Katherine Hicks and Yvonne "Bonnie" (Cecil) Tanner;
21 grandchildren, Pam (Freddie) Young, Kathy Bowen, Dana Hicks,
22 Sharon McAllister, and Charlotte Edwards, 12
23 great-grandchildren, and numerous nieces and nephews; now
24 therefore,
25 BE IT RESOLVED BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF
26 THE LEGISLATURE OF ALABAMA, That we are deeply saddened by the
27 death of Belle Farmer of Attalla, Alabama, and by copy of this
Page 1

1 resolution, we extend heartfelt sympathy to her family and
2 friends, whose sorrow we sincerely share.
