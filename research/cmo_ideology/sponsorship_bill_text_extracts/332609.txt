8 MOURNING THE DEATH OF MR. ARCHIE L. FARMER. 
9  
10 WHEREAS, with profound sorrow and regret, we receive
11 news of the death of Mr. Archie L. Farmer of Fruithurst,
12 Alabama, on January 28, 2011, at the age of 83 years; and
13 WHEREAS, the passing of Mr. Farmer leaves a void in
14 the lives of his loving family and his many friends; and 
15 WHEREAS, a native and lifelong resident of Cleburne
16 County, he was a member of Fruithurst Baptist Church, where he
17 taught Sunday School for 30 years; he was retired from a
18 career as a civil servant at Fort McClellan; and 
19 WHEREAS, he is survived by his wife, Dean Farmer;
20 son, Stanley Farmer; and daughter, Jenny Armstrong; preceding
21 him in death was his sister, Hazel Fuqua; and 
22 WHEREAS, Mr. Farmer was a valued member of the
23 community; although his presence will be missed, his memory
24 will be cherished in the hearts and minds of those who were
25 fortunate to know him; now therefore, 
26 BE IT RESOLVED BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF
27 THE LEGISLATURE OF ALABAMA, That the death of Mr. Archie L.
Page 1

1 Farmer is recorded with deep sadness, and this resolution is
2 offered in tribute to his life, along with heartfelt sympathy
3 to his grieving family.
