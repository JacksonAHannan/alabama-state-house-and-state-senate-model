SYNOPSIS:          Existing law does not require a business8
entity or public employer in the State of Alabama9
to verify the legal status of a new employee when10
hiring a new employee.11
This bill would require a business entity or12
public employer in the State of Alabama to verify13
the legal status of a new employee through the14
federal E-verify program or any other method that15
reasonably determines the legal status of the new16
employee and would provide for the suspension of17
the business licenses of a business entity who18
violates this act.19
