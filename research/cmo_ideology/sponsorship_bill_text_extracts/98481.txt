SYNOPSIS:          Under existing law, legend drugs dispensed8
to a patient at a hospital, nursing facility,9
assisted living facility, or hospice may be donated10
and transferred to charitable clinics to be used by11
charitable patients without charge if certain12
conditions are met.13
This bill would allow legend drugs to be14
dispensed by prescription but not administered to15
patients at certain health care facilities to be16
donated and transferred to charitable clinics under17
certain conditions; to authorize the drugs to be18
transferred in the same manner to charitable19
pharmacies, as defined, to be dispensed to20
charitable patients; and to further authorize21
samples given to physicians to be donated and22
transferred to charitable clinics and charitable23
pharmacies.24
