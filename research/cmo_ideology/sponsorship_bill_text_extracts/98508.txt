SYNOPSIS:          Under existing law, there is no express8
provision that makes it unlawful for a person, by9
coercion or deception, to cause another person to10
work or perform services having financial value or11
require that person to perform certain sexual12
activities.13
This bill would make it unlawful for a14
person, by coercion or deception, to cause another15
person to work or perform services having financial16
value or require that person to perform certain17
sexual activities.18
This bill would provide penalties.19
This bill would exempt a corporation unless20
the corporation either authorized, requested,21
commanded, performed, or committed the offense of22
human trafficking on behalf of the corporation or23
engaged in a pattern of human trafficking that an24
agent of the company knew or should have known was25
occurring.26
