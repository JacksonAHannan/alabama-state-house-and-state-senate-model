ENROLLED, House Joint Resolution,2
DESIGNATING THE BRIDGE PRESENTLY KNOWN AS SHOAL3
CREEK BRIDGE ON ALABAMA HIGHWAY 72 BETWEEN KILLEN AND FLORENCE4
THE DISABLED AMERICAN VETERANS MEMORIAL BRIDGE.5
 6
WHEREAS, it is the desire of the Alabama Legislature7
to honor the service and sacrifice of America's disabled8
military veterans at every opportunity, we are pleased to name9
a bridge on Alabama Highway 72 in their honor; and10
WHEREAS, those who go in harm's way wearing the11
uniforms of the United States Armed Services engage in a noble12
pursuit, risking all in service to their fellow Americans; and13
WHEREAS, America's disabled veterans, who bear the14
physical as well as symbolic scars of their service to a15
higher good are deserving of our most solemn and heartfelt16
veneration; now therefore,17
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF ALABAMA, BOTH18
HOUSES THEREOF CONCURRING, That the bridge presently known as19
Shoal Creek Bridge on Alabama Highway 72 between Killen and20
Florence is hereby designated the Disabled American Veterans21
Memorial Bridge and that the proper officials are requested to22
place and maintain markers designating the Disabled American23
Veterans Memorial Bridge as provided in this resolution.24
