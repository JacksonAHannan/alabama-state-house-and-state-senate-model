8 SYNOPSIS:         This bill would provide for the issuance of
9 a personalized in memory license plate honoring
10 deceased family members. 
11 This bill would provide that a motor vehicle
12 owner who is a resident of the state could request
13 a personalized in memory license motor vehicle tag
14 upon payment of an additional fee of $25.
15 This bill would provide for distribution of
16 the additional tag fee to the State General Fund. 
17  
18 A BILL
19 TO BE ENTITLED
20 AN ACT
21  
22 To provide for the issuance of a personalized in
23 memory license plate honoring deceased family members; to
24 provide for an additional fee for the license plate; and to
25 provide for the distribution of the proceeds of the additional
26 fee.
27 BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF ALABAMA:
Page 1

1 Section 1. (a) Notwithstanding Sections 32-6-64,
2 32-6-67, and 32-6-68 of the Code of Alabama 1975, upon
3 application to the judge of probate or license commissioner,
4 compliance with motor vehicle registration and licensing laws,
5 payment of regular fees required by law for license plates for
6 private passenger or pleasure motor vehicles, and payment of
7 an additional annual fee of twenty-five dollars ($25), owners
8 of motor vehicles who are residents of Alabama shall upon
9 request be issued a personalized in memory license plate
10 honoring deceased family members.
11 (b) These plates shall be valid for five years and
12 may then be replaced with either a conventional, personalized,
13 or distinctive license plate.
14 (c) Payment of required license fees and taxes for
15 the years during which a new plate is not issued shall be
16 evidenced as provided in Section 32-6-63 of the Code of
17 Alabama 1975. 
18 (d) The plates shall be issued, printed, and
19 processed like other distinctive or personalized plates
20 provided for by law.
21 (e) The State Revenue Department shall prescribe for
22 identification purposes any number or symbol necessary to the
23 language requested by a person for an in memory tag issued.
24 (f) The additional revenues derived from the sales
25 of the distinctive motor vehicle license plates shall be
26 distributed monthly to the State General Fund.
