2 ENROLLED, House Joint Resolution,
3 NAMING A PORTION OF US HIGHWAY 72 AT MILE MARKER 18
4 TO THE ALABAMA/MISSISSIPPI STATE LINE THE FRANKIE LEE WALLACE
5 HIGHWAY.
6  
7 WHEREAS, First Lieutenant Frankie Lee Wallace, of
8 Cherokee, Alabama, while serving in Vietnam, was leading a
9 patrol on a routine search of several villages when his squad
10 was suddenly engaged by small arms and automatic weapons fire
11 from a Viet Cong squad; and
12 WHEREAS, upon encountering hostile fire, First
13 Lieutenant Wallace led an assault on the positions
14 successfully forcing the Viet Cong to withdraw; and
15 WHEREAS, First Lieutenant Wallace then led pursuit
16 of the retreating Viet Cong until the insurgent force joined
17 with a well-entrenched platoon size force; and
18 WHEREAS, First Lieutenant Wallace then directed
19 effective artillery fire and air strikes on the Viet Cong
20 platoon's positions; and
21 WHEREAS, First Lieutenant Wallace then led another
22 assault and was met by intense automatic weapons and mortar
23 fire, forcing him to withdraw; and
24 WHEREAS, during the withdrawal, a member of
25 Wallace's squad fell wounded, and, Wallace, without
Page 1

HJR13
1 hesitation, ran into an open rice paddy to aid his wounded
2 comrade, receiving a wound to the leg before reaching him; and
3 WHEREAS, First Lieutenant Wallace, unmindful of his
4 wound, continued to approach the stricken solider, killing two
5 Viet Cong who were also attempting to reach the wounded man;
6 and
7 WHEREAS, despite being fully exposed to intense Viet
8 Cong fire concentrated upon him, First Lieutenant Wallace
9 provided aid to the wounded solider and attempted to assist
10 him to safety, receiving a mortal wound by hostile automatic
11 weapons fire while doing so; and
12 WHEREAS, First Lieutenant Wallace's leadership,
13 courage, and inspiring example were instrumental in saving the
14 life of the injured soldier; and
15 WHEREAS, First Lieutenant Wallace was posthumously
16 awarded the Silver Star for his leadership and unimpeachable
17 valor displayed against a numerically superior hostile force,
18 reflecting great credit upon himself, his unit, and the United
19 States Army; now therefore,
20 BE IT RESOLVED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF ALABAMA, BOTH
21 HOUSES THEREOF CONCURRING, That we hereby designate the
22 section of US Highway 72 beginning at Mile Marker 18 and
23 continuing West to the Alabama/Mississippi State Line as the
24 "Frankie Lee Wallace Highway" in honor and memory of First
25 Lieutenant Frankie Lee Wallace.
