2 ENROLLED, House Joint Resolution,
3 RECOGNIZING RED BAY HOSPITAL FOR 50 YEARS OF SERVICE
4 TO THE COMMUNITY.
5  
6 WHEREAS, highest commendations and congratulations
7 are herein proudly presented to Red Bay Hospital on the
8 occasion of its 50th year serving the Red Bay community and
9 surrounding areas; and
10 WHEREAS, since its inception in 1967, Red Bay
11 Hospital has been an integral part of the well-being of the
12 people of Northwest Alabama and Northeast Mississippi; and
13 WHEREAS, in 2005, Red Bay Hospital was awarded the
14 status of Critical Access Hospital by the State of Alabama and
15 the Center for Medicare and Medicaid Services; and 
16 WHEREAS, Red Bay Hospital is one of only four
17 Critical Access hospitals in the State of Alabama, which means
18 that the citizens the hospital serves can count on access to
19 quality health care within a reasonable distance from their
20 homes; and
21 WHEREAS, Red Bay Hospital is a non-profit hospital
22 owned and operated by the Colbert County-Northwest Alabama
23 Health Care Authority; and

1 WHEREAS, Red Bay Hospital, along with its dedicated
2 staff, is committed to providing cost-effective care with
3 access for all who are in need; and
4 WHEREAS, Red Bay Hospital is proud to display its
5 new logo, "50 Years of Healing, 50 Years of Caring"; now
6 therefore,
7 BE IT RESOLVED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF ALABAMA, BOTH
8 HOUSES THEREOF CONCURRING, That Red Bay Hospital is highly
9 honored and commended, and this resolution is offered in
10 deepest admiration and appreciation, along with best wishes
11 for all future endeavors.
