13 Relating to Lauderdale and Colbert Counties; to
14 amend Section 45-17-91.23, Code of Alabama 1975, relating to
15 the Shoals Economic Development Fund; to provide that a
16 portion of the Shoals Economic Development Fund may be for
17 workforce training and economic development programs at the
18 University of North Alabama.
19 BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF ALABAMA:
20 Section 1. Section 45-17-91.23, Code of Alabama
21 1975, is amended to read as follows:
22 "§45-17-91.23.
23 "(a) There is created the Shoals Economic
24 Development Fund, which shall be a special or trust fund or
25 account of the committee, and which shall be administered in
26 accordance with this subpart.

1 "(b)(1) The authority may, at any time and from time
2 to time, request that the committee authorize and approve the
3 expenditure or appropriation of moneys on deposit in the
4 Shoals Economic Development Fund, but solely for purposes
5 authorized in subsection (c). Any such request may specify
6 that such moneys shall be expended by, or appropriated

8 "a. The authority itself.
9 "b. Either of the counties, or any city or town

1 "(d) Any moneys authorized, pursuant to this
2 subpart, to be expended by either the authority or any other
3 recipient shall, if not expended for the purpose or purposes
4 approved by the committee, be promptly returned to and
5 deposited into the Shoals Economic Development Fund.
6 "(e) It is the express intent of the Legislature
7 that all expenditures of moneys at any time on deposit in the
8 Shoals Economic Development Fund be made to promote the
9 economic development of the citizens of each of the counties
10 and every city or town therein, to create and provide for the
11 creation of non-retail job opportunities that support salaries
12 at or above the estimated entry wage per occupation, as
13 established by the most recent wage survey of the State of
14 Alabama, and to enable the committee and the participating
15 governments to support (1) the establishment and promotion of
16 business and industry in the Shoals area, (2) the development
17 of trade and commerce in the Shoals area, (3) the furtherance
18 of the use of the agricultural products and natural resources
19 of the State of Alabama, and (4) the development and
20 preservation of such resources. However, the Legislature
21 recognizes and acknowledges that the authority and other
22 potential recipients of moneys on deposit in the Shoals
23 Economic Development Fund may be empowered by law to engage in
24 activities, or to perform functions, for purposes that are
25 different from, or that are unrelated to, the purposes for
26 which such moneys are authorized by this subpart to be
27 expended. It is therefore also the intent of the Legislature
Page 6

1 that no moneys on deposit in the Shoals Economic Development
2 Fund be expended by, or appropriated to, either the authority
