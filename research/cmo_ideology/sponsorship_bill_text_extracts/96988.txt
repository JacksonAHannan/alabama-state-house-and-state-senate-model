Unexplained Infant Death Investigation Act, to9
provide for investigating suspected cases of sudden10
unexplained infant death (SUID) of infants less11
than one year of age.12
This bill would create the Alabama Sudden13
Unexplained Infant Death Investigation (SUIDI)14
Team, comprised of certain state officials and15
representatives of certain state agencies, to16
provide for the development and maintenance of17

This bill would provide for the coordination20

emergency personnel in investigating suspected23
cases of SUID.24
This bill would provide certain persons and25
