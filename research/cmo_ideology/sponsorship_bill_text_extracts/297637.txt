SYNOPSIS:         This bill would provide that if the state8
makes a commitment to a company to provide economic9
development funds as an incentive to build or10
expand in this state, the state and the company11
must enter into a written mutually acceptable12
contract within five years after the date of the13
commitment in order for the commitment to be valid.14
This bill would provide that any commitment15
already made by the state where the company and the16
state do not enter into a written mutually17
acceptable contract within five years after the18
date of the commitment or one year after the19
effective date of this act is void.20
