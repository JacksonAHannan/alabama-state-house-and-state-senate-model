SYNOPSIS:          Under existing law, a person who assaults a8
peace officer with an intent to prevent the peace9
officer from performing a lawful duty and who10
causes physical injury to the peace officer or11
another person is guilty of assault in the second12
degree. Assault in the second degree is a Class C13
felony. The courts have held that an off-duty peace14
officer employed by a private entity is not a peace15
officer unless the off-duty peace officer has16
witnessed a crime.17
This bill would provide that an off-duty18
peace officer employed by a private entity would be19
considered a peace officer and to be performing a20
lawful duty in his or her approved uniform while21
off duty with the approval of his or her employing22
agency.23
