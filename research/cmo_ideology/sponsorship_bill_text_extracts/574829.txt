SYNOPSIS:         Under existing law, the Retirement Systems8
of Alabama are governed by Boards of Control9
comprised of active and retired public employees10
who serve by virtue of their election or in their11
official capacities by virtue of their elected or12
appointed position. The State Employees' Retirement13
System and the Teachers' Retirement System each14
have similarly comprised Boards of Control.15
Under existing law, the day-to-day16
operations of all of the Retirement Systems of17
Alabama are managed by a secretary-treasurer who18
serves at the pleasure of each Board of Control.19
Under existing law, the Board of Control for20
the Employees' Retirement System, by majority vote,21
may make decisions regarding the operation and22
functioning of the Employees' Retirement System and23
the Judicial Retirement Fund and the Board of24
Control of the Teachers' Retirement System, by25
majority vote, may make decisions regarding the26
operation of the Teachers' Retirement System. The27
Page 1

Board of Control of the Employees' Retirement1
Systems of Alabama, by resolution, has provided2
that all investment decisions of the3
secretary-treasurer must be approved by a4
three-person investment committee.5
This bill would give the Secretary-Treasurer6
of the Retirement Systems of Alabama the authority7
to make investment decisions regarding moneys in8
the Employees' Retirement System, the Teachers'9
Retirement System, and the Judicial Retirement Fund10
without approval of the Board of Control of either11
the Employees' Retirement System or the Teachers'12
Retirement System.13
