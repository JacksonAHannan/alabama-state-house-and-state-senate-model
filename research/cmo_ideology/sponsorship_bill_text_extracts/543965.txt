2 ENROLLED, An Act,
3 Relating to Cherokee County; providing that the
4 sheriff may provide to a law enforcement officer of the
5 sheriff's office, upon retirement, a retired badge and service
6 pistol.
7 BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF ALABAMA:
8 Section 1. Any person employed as a law enforcement
9 officer by the Sheriff's Office of Cherokee County for a
10 period of 10 years or more who retires from the office in good
11 standing may receive from the office, without cost, a retired
12 badge, a retired commission card, and his or her service
13 pistol, provided the pistol is furnished by the office.
14 Section 2. All laws or parts of laws which conflict
15 with this act are repealed.
16 Section 3. This act shall become effective
17 immediately following its passage and approval by the
18 Governor, or its otherwise becoming law.
