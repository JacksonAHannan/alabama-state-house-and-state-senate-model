SYNOPSIS:          Under existing law, a person convicted of8
capital murder is sentenced to the term of life9
without the possibility of parole or death.10
This bill would provide that if the person11
convicted of a capital offense was a first-time12
felony offender who had no prior felony convictions13
at the time of the capital offense and the14
defendant meets certain other requirements, the15
sentencing/presiding judge would be required to16
reduce the defendant's sentence from life without17
the possibility of parole to life with the18
possibility of parole.19
This bill would provide for retroactive20
review of offenders sentenced prior to the21
effective date of this act.22
