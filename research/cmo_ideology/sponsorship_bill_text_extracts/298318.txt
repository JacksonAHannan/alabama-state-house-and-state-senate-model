2 ENROLLED, An Act,
3 To provide an incentive for job creation in Alabama;
4 and to make legislative findings regarding the purpose of this
5 act.
6 BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF ALABAMA:
7 Section 1. This act may be cited as the "Full
8 Employment Act of 2011."
9 Section 2. For purposes of this act, a small
10 business is defined as a business that employs 50 or fewer
11 employees on the effective date of this act.
12 Section 3. The Legislature finds and determines, in
13 accordance with its goal, shared by Governor Robert Bentley,
14 of achieving statistical full employment for Alabama's
15 citizens, that the Legislature intends to foster an optimal
16 environment for job growth throughout the state.
17 Section 4. (a) An Alabama income and financial
18 institution excise tax credit is hereby established for small
19 businesses that create new jobs paying more than ten dollars
20 ($10) per hour. The credit shall equal one thousand dollars
21 ($1,000) and shall be available in the tax year during which
22 the employee has completed 12 months of consecutive
23 employment.
24 (b) The employer must have a net increase in the
25 total number of full time employees in Alabama on the last

18 (d) A financial institution shall be allowed to

21 (d) (e) The income tax credit provided in this
