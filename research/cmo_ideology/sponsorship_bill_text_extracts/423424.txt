2 ENROLLED, An Act,
3 Relating to Cleburne County; providing for the
4 assessment and collection of a drug and violent crime
5 reduction fee payable to the Cleburne County Sheriff's Office
6 and the Cleburne County Office of the Circuit Clerk in all
7 criminal cases; and providing for the disbursement of the
8 funds.
9 BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF ALABAMA:
10 Section 1. In addition to all other fees and court
11 costs, in all juvenile, traffic, criminal, and quasi-criminal
12 cases in the juvenile, district, and circuit courts in
13 Cleburne County, a docket fee in the amount of five dollars
14 ($5), hereinafter referred to as a drug and violent crime
15 reduction fee, shall be assessed in each case. The fees, when
16 collected, shall be distributed monthly as follows: One dollar
17 ($1) per case to the Cleburne County Office of the Circuit
18 Clerk and four dollars ($4) per case to the Cleburne County
19 Sheriff's Office.
20 Section 2. The drug and violent crime reduction fee
21 shall be collected in all criminal cases where the defendant
22 is adjudged guilty, a bond forfeited, a penalty imposed, or
23 where there is issued any alias or capias warrant of arrest.
24 The drug and violent crime reduction fee shall be in addition
25 to and not in lieu of any other fees or costs. The drug and

1 violent crime reduction fee shall not be waived or remitted
2 unless the defendant proves to the reasonable satisfaction of
3 the sentencing judge that the defendant is not capable of
4 paying the fee within the reasonably foreseeable future.
5 Section 3. The drug and violent crime reduction fee
6 disbursed to the sheriff may be expended by the sheriff for
7 any and all expenses incurred by the Calhoun/Cleburne Drug and
8 Violent Crime Task Force law enforcement and in the discharge
9 of the duties of the task force. Should the Calhoun/Cleburne
10 Drug and Violent Crime Task Force ever be dissolved and
11 disbanded by the board of directors, that portion of the fees
12 distributed to the sheriff's department by this act from that
13 time forward shall remain with the sheriff's department to be
14 used for the expenses and operation of that department.
15 Section 4. This act shall become effective
16 immediately following its passage and approval by the
17 Governor, or its otherwise becoming law.
