8 SYNOPSIS:         Currently there is projected to be a
9 shortage of funds to pay the full tuition and fee
10 costs to the beneficiaries of the remaining active
11 PACT contract holders.
12 This bill will provide that certain assets
13 in the PACT Trust will be distributed to the
14 several universities and colleges in the state and
15 that certain funds would be retained in the fund to
16 be available for private college or out-of-state
17 tuition if selected by the PACT beneficiary. 
18 This bill would also provide that future
19 appropriations from the Education Trust Fund to the
20 PACT programs would be distributed to the several
21 universities and colleges in the same manner as the
22 assets transferred. Any state university or college
23 shall allow a PACT beneficiary who is accepted for
24 admission to receive credit for all tuition and
25 fees as under the terms of the PACT contracts which
26 existed prior to the passage of Act 2010-725. Any
27 PACT beneficiary electing a private or out-of-state
Page 1

1 institution shall receive the amounts as determined
2 according to the methods in place prior to the
3 passage of Act 2010-725.
