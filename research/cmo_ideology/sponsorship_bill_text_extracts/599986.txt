8 SYNOPSIS:         Under existing law, lotteries and gift
9 enterprises are prohibited.
10 This bill would propose an amendment to
11 Section 65 of the Constitution of Alabama of 1901,
12 to establish an Alabama Lottery to fund certain
13 Education Trust Fund programs, to create an Alabama
14 Lottery Corporation to implement and to regulate
15 the operation of such lottery, and to prohibit the
16 operation of casinos in the State.
17 The amendment dedicates the net proceeds
18 from the lottery to the Education Trust Fund for
19 resource officers and scholarships. The amendment
20 provides that lottery proceeds will not supplant or
21 replace existing education revenues, establishes
22 the Lottery Trust Fund, and authorizes the
23 Legislature to pass general laws to implement the
24 amendment.
