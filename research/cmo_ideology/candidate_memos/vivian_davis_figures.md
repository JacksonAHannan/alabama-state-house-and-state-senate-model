# Vivian Davis Figures

## Why she matters

Vivian Davis Figures recorded about `+16.7` OOF CMO in Senate District 33 in 2010. She is a strong progressive counterexample with unusually good pre-election evidence.

## Ideological evidence

Her pre-election Shor–McCarty score places her around the 23rd conservative percentile of the Alabama Democratic Senate caucus. In February 2010 she sponsored SB401, which would have imposed escalating penalties for negligently storing firearms where minors could gain access. She also had a long pre-2010 record of pursuing comprehensive restrictions on smoking in workplaces and public accommodations.

These are campaign-time governing positions, not later inferences. They place Figures on the regulatory and gun-safety side of the caucus rather than among culturally conservative crossover Democrats.

## Competing explanation

Figures had represented the majority-Black Mobile district since 1997 after succeeding her late husband, former Senate president pro tempore Michael Figures. Incumbency, family political networks, and Black down-ballot coalition loyalty are powerful alternative mechanisms.

## Interpretation

Figures reinforces the claim that ideological mechanisms differ by coalition. Her overperformance cannot plausibly be attributed to moving right on guns. It fits the same majority-Black community-network mechanism seen with Barbara Boyd and Barbara Drummond.
