# Darrell Turner comparison memo

## Matched-comparison role

Darrell Turner is the automated 2014 comparison for Larry Means. Means recorded
about `+29.1` OOF CMO and Turner about `+4.4`, a gap of roughly 25 points.

## Ideological profile

Turner ran as an economic-populist, labor-aligned Democrat. He was a longtime
Plumbers and Pipefitters business representative, emphasized working families
and public education, criticized the Alabama Accountability Act, and presented
compromise and transparent government as central themes. Available sources do
not establish his positions on abortion or guns, so those remain unobserved.

This makes the pair more useful than a generic liberal-versus-conservative
comparison. Means and Turner shared working-class and public-education themes;
Means additionally had a documented pro-gun cultural profile and a powerful
Attalla personal vote.

## Competing explanations

Campaign resources explain a meaningful portion of the pair's gap. Turner was
at an extreme spending disadvantage, while Means substantially outspent his
Republican opponent. Adjusting for resources reduces the CMO difference from
about 25 to 13 points, though it does not eliminate it.

Turner also faced a late complaint alleging that he filed his statement of
economic interests months after the deadline. The report questioned his ballot
eligibility but did not document a final adjudication, so this is coded as
campaign context, not established misconduct.

## Interpretation

The pair is consistent with cultural differentiation and local reputation
helping Means, but money and late negative coverage are substantial rival
explanations. It should not be presented as a clean estimate of ideology.
