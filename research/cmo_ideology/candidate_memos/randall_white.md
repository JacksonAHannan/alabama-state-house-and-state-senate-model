# Randall White

## Why he matters

Randall White recorded about `+21.6` OOF CMO in the open 2010 House District 26 race while losing to Republican Kerry Rich. His candidacy is a direct example of the older culturally conservative, locally rooted Democratic type.

## Evidence

A contemporaneous Democratic campaign blog called White highly conservative, described him as a lifelong district resident, and identified him as a recently retired adult-education supervisor. It specifically argued that the Republican effort to label him liberal lacked credibility with local voters.

This source is temporally appropriate but openly partisan and supplies no issue-by-issue positions. The broad conservative classification is therefore provisional and low confidence. The local-root and educator facts are more concrete, but still require independent corroboration.

## Interpretation

White supports the district-congruent differentiation hypothesis, but he should not anchor an article claim until a candidate questionnaire, local newspaper profile, advertisement, or endorsement record verifies his positions. His case also bundles ideology with lifelong residency and an education career.
