# Jeff McLaughlin

## Why he matters

Jeff McLaughlin recorded about `+37.7` Total OOF CMO in House District 27 in
2014, one of the largest scores in the cohort. Resource-adjusted CMO remains
about `+36.5`, and he raised only about `$13,455` against Will Ainsworth's
roughly `$113,956`.

## Ideological valence

McLaughlin had served in the House from 2001 through 2010, so his roll-call
record predates the election being explained. His Shor–McCarty score places him
around the 92nd conservative percentile of the Alabama Democratic House caucus.
Combined with the contemporary campaign description, that supports a conservative
*relative-caucus* classification, while avoiding a stronger code based on the
roll-call percentile alone.

The campaign record is more multidimensional. His 2013 announcement emphasized
education, infrastructure, protecting TVA resources, opposition to extreme
partisanship, and honesty in government. Election-night coverage described his
platform as education and representation without special-interest money. His
earlier legislative record included repeated efforts to ban PAC-to-PAC transfers
and a 2010 proposal for nonpartisan judicial elections.

These positions establish reform and public-institution themes, but they do not
justify separate gun, abortion, labor, or economic codes without more specific
evidence.

## Competing explanations

McLaughlin was not a typical challenger. He had represented HD-27 for a decade,
was a Guntersville attorney and former teacher, and had extensive local civic
ties. His opponent substantially outraised him and ran as a business-oriented
outsider. McLaughlin's result is therefore consistent with a durable personal
vote surviving a party defeat, not merely with ideological positioning.

## Interpretation

McLaughlin supports ideological congruence as one part of the white-district
story, but his campaign brand was not simply cultural conservatism. It joined a
conservative relative-caucus voting record to education, TVA, clean-government,
and anti-special-interest appeals. His long prior service and local professional
network remain powerful competing mechanisms.
