# John “Jody” Letson

## Alabama identity and biography

John “Jody” Letson was the Democratic candidate for Alabama House District 7 in 2014 and had previously represented that district from the 1990s through 2010. Vote Smart identifies him as a Lawrence County native and Hillsboro resident and records earlier service as a Lawrence County commissioner.

Its biography reports that Letson attended Calhoun Community College, graduated from Courtland High School, worked for General Motors, operated Letson’s Grocery, and farmed cotton and corn with Letson Brothers Farm. It also lists service connected to the Lawrence County Historical Commission, industrial development, regional government, mental health, and youth organizations.

## Research interpretation

The source explicitly connects the biography to the 2014 Alabama House District 7 candidate, substantially reducing the same-name risk. Occupational and local-service history may support a personal-vote explanation, but policy conclusions require campaign or roll-call evidence.
