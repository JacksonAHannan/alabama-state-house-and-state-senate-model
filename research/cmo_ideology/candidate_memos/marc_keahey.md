# Marc Keahey

## Alabama identity and biography

George Marc Keahey was the Democratic candidate for Alabama Senate District 22 in 2010. He had represented Alabama House District 65 after his 2006 election and won the 2009 special election for Senate District 22. Contemporary Alabama Public Radio reporting places him in the District 22 special election, while the certified 2010 result identifies Keahey as the Democratic Senate candidate.

Keahey is an attorney. Public biographies report a University of Alabama business degree and a Cumberland School of Law J.D., followed by work as a public defender, assistant district attorney, and private lawyer. His state legislative committee work included agriculture and conservation, business and labor, fiscal responsibility, and judiciary.

## Research interpretation

The legislative service and professional details clearly concern the Alabama candidate rather than a same-name person. Committee and occupational history provide context but do not by themselves establish ideological positions. Recorded roll calls should be the behavioral evidence.
