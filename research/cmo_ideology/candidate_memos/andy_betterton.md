# Andy Betterton

## Why he matters

Andy Betterton recorded about `+27.5` OOF CMO in House District 2 in 2014, despite losing to Republican incumbent Lynn Greer. He is a useful white-north-Alabama case combining a progressive economic platform with substantial local elected-service experience.

## What the evidence supports

Betterton's biography describes two terms on the Florence City Board of Education followed by a second term on the Florence City Council, along with extensive civic-board service. The site's WordPress API dates this biography to August 12, 2014 and its last modification to August 22, so the local-embeddedness evidence is contemporaneous.

A House District 2 issues page favors public education, opposes using public tax dollars for private schools, and supports Medicaid expansion. Those are economically progressive positions. Although the rendered page now carries a later footer, the site's WordPress API dates publication to August 12, 2014 and the last modification to August 21, 2014. The economic-ideology code is therefore temporally eligible for the 2014 analysis.

## Interpretation

Betterton is direct evidence against a simple conservative-moderation account: a Democrat could substantially outperform in a white north-Alabama district while publicly supporting Medicaid expansion and public-school investment. His long school-board and city-council tenure is also a powerful competing explanation, so the case supports an interaction between ideological commitments and local credibility rather than an ideology-only mechanism.
