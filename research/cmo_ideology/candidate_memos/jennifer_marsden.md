# Jennifer Marsden comparison memo

## Matched-comparison role

Jennifer Marsden is the automated comparison for Henry White in 2014. White's
OOF CMO is about `+29.7`; Marsden's is about `-23.2`. Both were Democratic
challengers to Republican House incumbents in heavily Republican, mostly white
districts, but the comparison is descriptive rather than causal.

## Ideological and professional profile

Marsden's contemporaneous platform was economically progressive: health care
for all Alabamians, strong public education, vocational training, small-business
incubators, and opposition to corporate welfare and budget-straining tax breaks.
She was a family physician and Army veteran and signed a cross-partisan
anti-corruption pledge.

Marsden made Medicaid expansion a particularly clear campaign issue. That does
not create a clean ideological contrast with her opponent, however. Republican
incumbent Steve Clouse also said before the election that he favored expansion
if Alabama's contribution remained capped at ten percent. The difference was
therefore one of commitment and framing, not a simple expansion-versus-rejection
divide.

## Why the pair matters

This pair is consistent with conservative ideological congruence helping White,
but it also exposes major competing explanations. White had a lifetime in
Limestone County, 36 years in its schools, service as superintendent and city
council member, and a prior term in the Legislature. Marsden had lived in the
Wiregrass for seven years and had no comparable elected network. The CMO gap
cannot be assigned to ideology without separating local roots, opponent quality,
campaign strength, and district-specific partisan change.

Opponent incumbency is especially important. Clouse had represented House
District 93 since 1994 and chaired the General Fund Ways and Means Committee.
His two-party share was about 77.7 percent in 2010 and 76.7 percent against
Marsden in 2014. Marsden therefore performed about as well as the district's
previous Democratic nominee, rather than suffering a distinctive collapse that
can readily be attributed to her platform.
