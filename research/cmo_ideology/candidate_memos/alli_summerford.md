# Alli Summerford

## Alabama identity and biography

Alli Summerford was the 2018 Democratic candidate for Alabama House District 48, covering parts of Jefferson and Shelby counties. The Alabama Democratic Party certification and certified general-election results confirm that electoral identity.

Contemporary profiles describe Summerford as a Mountain Brook resident, first-time candidate, mother, and founder of a web-design and digital-marketing business operated for roughly two decades. She earned a bachelor’s degree from the University of Tennessee and an MBA focused on new-venture analysis. Reported civic work included a women’s shelter board, library support, Rotary exchange service, and founding a local track-club booster organization.

Her campaign emphasized public education, healthcare access, safer communities, roads, local-government flexibility, government ethics, and environmental protection. These are campaign-time statements and therefore usable as evidence of her presented political profile in 2018.
