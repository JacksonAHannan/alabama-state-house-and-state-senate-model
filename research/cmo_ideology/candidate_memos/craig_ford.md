# Craig Ford

## Why he matters

Craig Ford recorded about `+23.7` OOF CMO in House District 28 in 2014, with
almost the same district-grouped estimate and about `+22.9` after adjusting for
campaign resources. He is one of the clearest examples of explicit distance
from the national Democratic brand.

That production score is not geographically stable. Refitting the model across
the audited allocation regimes yields a range of roughly `+8.1` to `+23.7`.
Ford remains positive, but the production geography places him at the upper end
of his plausible historical score range.

## Ideological valence

Ford's pre-election Shor--McCarty score was `-0.152`, near the 89th conservative
percentile of the contemporary Alabama Democratic House caucus. After leaving
the party to run for Senate as an independent in 2018, he described himself as
aligned with conservative principles and estranged from national Democrats.
Vote Smart later recorded a 92 percent NRA candidate-position rating and a 100
percent Gun Owners of America rating.

That label still conceals an important issue bundle. In the same 2018 interview,
Ford advocated full public-school funding, pre-K and teacher raises, repeal of
the Alabama Accountability Act, Medicaid expansion, and stronger mental-health
funding. Cultural conservatism coexisted with positions conventionally located
on the economic left.

## Interpretation

Ford strongly supports the *differentiation* part of the working thesis, but he
does not support a one-dimensional move-right story. His coalition appeal was a
combination of conservative cultural identity, economic-populist services, a
long local record, and explicit independence from party leadership. The most
detailed issue interview is retrospective to the scored 2014 election, so it
should corroborate the roll-call score rather than substitute for a
contemporaneous 2014 platform.
