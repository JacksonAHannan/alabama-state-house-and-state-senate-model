# Michael J. Gladden comparison memo

## Matched-comparison role

Michael Gladden is the automated comparison for Johnny Mack Morrow in 2014.
Gladden's OOF CMO is about `-8.2`, versus Morrow's `+27.7`. Both ran in
majority-white, lower-college north-Alabama districts, but they were radically
different candidate types.

## What can be established

Gladden was not a political novice. He had served as Etowah County coroner
since 2006, briefly served as sheriff after the incumbent's death, worked as a
funeral-home manager, and had a long public-service résumé. Surviving campaign
material emphasized public education, job creation, fiscal restraint, and a
proposal preventing convicted rapists from claiming custody or visitation.
That record does not supply comparable evidence on abortion, guns, labor, or
the other cultural issues documented for Morrow.

## Why the pair is limited

Morrow brought 24 years of legislative service and a record of district-level
projects. Gladden challenged Republican incumbent Becky Nordgren, who had
defeated a Democratic incumbent in 2010. Gladden's 36.2 percent was also well
below the prior incumbent Democrat's 48 percent under the earlier district
configuration, making redistricting and incumbency important unheld factors.
The pair can illustrate Morrow's distinctive bundle, but cannot estimate the
effect of that bundle.
