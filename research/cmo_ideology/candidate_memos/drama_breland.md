# Drama Breland

## Why she matters

Drama Breland recorded about `+22.7` OOF CMO in the open 2010 House District 8 race against Terri Collins. She narrowly lost during the Republican wave.

## Evidence status

The official Morgan County ballot verifies her identity and Democratic nomination. A June 17, 2010 Hartselle Enquirer report on her appointment as the Democratic nominee supplies a concise contemporaneous platform. Breland listed public education, college affordability, senior citizens, and job development as her priorities if elected. The county Democratic chair separately described her as passionate about education and committed to community service; that description is an endorsement, not a candidate statement.

Later official and biographical material identifies Breland as the wife of longtime Decatur municipal judge and civic leader David Breland and as part of a locally prominent judicial family. This suggests a potential familiarity and civic-network mechanism, but it is retrospective evidence and cannot establish campaign effects.

## Interpretation

Breland's recovered platform is conventionally Democratic and service-oriented rather than evidence of cultural conservatism. Her result could still reflect local networks, candidate quality, district partisanship not captured by the model, or opponent factors. She should be described as emphasizing education, affordability, seniors, and jobs; her positions on guns, abortion, and other polarizing state issues remain unknown.
