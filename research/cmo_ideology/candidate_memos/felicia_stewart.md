# Felicia Stewart

## Alabama identity and biography

Felicia Stewart was the 2018 Democratic candidate for Alabama House District 46 and a Mountain Brook resident. The Alabama Democratic Party certification confirms that identity. Contemporary voter guides describe her as a first-time candidate who returned to Alabama and became involved in nonprofit, hospital, education, and food-assistance work.

Stewart earned a bachelor’s degree in English from Spring Hill College and an MBA from Southern Methodist University. Reported professional experience included medical-device sales, sales management, and consulting for an LGBTQ-focused component fund at the Community Foundation of Greater Birmingham. Contemporary reporting identified her as openly gay and married to her wife, Christy.

## Research interpretation

These sources securely identify the Alabama candidate and establish professional, civic, and representational context. They do not substitute for detailed issue coding; campaign statements and subsequent recorded votes should remain separate evidence.
