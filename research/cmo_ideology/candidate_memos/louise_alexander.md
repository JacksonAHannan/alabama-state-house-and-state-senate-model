# Louise “Lulu” Alexander

## Why she matters

Louise Alexander recorded about `+7.4` Total OOF CMO in House District 56 in
2014. She is the matched comparison for Barbara Drummond, whose production CMO
is about `+29.9`. The pair has been classified as a case where the higher-CMO
candidate later governed more progressively, but that interpretation needs
strong qualifications.

## Ideological evidence

Alexander entered the Legislature in the election being explained. Her Shor–
McCarty score therefore reflects post-election voting and cannot represent what
voters knew in 2014. No contemporaneous issue platform sufficient to code guns,
abortion, economics, labor, or social ideology has been recovered.

Her ideology must remain unknown for causal interpretation. The later ideal point
can describe her governing record, but it cannot prove that Drummond's more
progressive profile caused the production-score gap.

## Local network and opponent

Alexander had served on the Bessemer City Council from 1998 through 2010 and
held unusually broad community ties through local businesses, funeral homes,
church, youth work, and alumni organizations. She also defeated incumbent
Democrat Lawrence McAdory in the 2014 primary.

Republican Darius Foster was an unusually active challenger in a majority-Black
district. NPR documented sustained door-to-door outreach, school-technology and
job-fair initiatives, national Republican attention, and at least some conversion
of prior Democratic supporters. FollowTheMoney reports about `$114,147` for
Foster; Alexander's total is not observed. By contrast, Drummond raised about
`$85,460` against an opponent with only about `$8,292`.

## Geography sensitivity

Alexander's CMO ranges from approximately `+7.4` to `+12.1` across geographic
allocations. Drummond ranges from about `+12.9` to `+29.9`. The production gap
is 22.5 points, but the most conservative cross-scenario gap is less than one
point. The direction barely survives; a substantively large gap does not.

## Interpretation

This pair cannot support a clean claim that progressive ideology produced more
overperformance. Both ideology measures are post-election, Drummond's score is
geographically unstable, and Alexander faced the stronger documented opponent.
The pair is better evidence for geography, resources, and opponent quality than
for ideological direction.
