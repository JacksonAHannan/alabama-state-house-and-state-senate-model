# Johnny Mack Morrow

## Why he matters

Johnny Mack Morrow recorded about `+27.7` OOF CMO in House District 18 in
2014. His automated comparison, Michael Gladden, recorded about `-8.2`, a gap
of roughly 35.9 points. Morrow is one of the clearest examples of the project's
white-rural ideological bundle, but also one of the clearest personal-vote
cases.

## Ideological valence

Morrow's 2018 Senate campaign described his existing record as pro-life,
pro-family, and pro-Second Amendment. The same profile emphasized defense of
public education, the Retirement Systems of Alabama, and opposition to shifting
public-school funds toward charter schools. That is strong evidence for his
2018 ideological presentation, but it should not simply be backdated four years.

The narrower pre-2014 record independently establishes two components. Morrow
sponsored an armed-school-security law allowing trained school personnel and
community volunteers to serve as reserve officers. He also advocated teacher
pay increases, educator recruitment and retention, AMSTI funding, and classroom
resources over school deregulation. His 2014 abortion position remains
unrecovered. The combined chronology supports a durable culturally conservative,
economically communitarian bundle while preserving the distinction between
what was documented before each election.

## Personal-vote evidence

By 2014 Morrow had represented the district for 24 years. Contemporary local
reporting credited him with constituent advocacy, a school-safety law, local
industrial and road projects, veterans programming, and returning TVA in-lieu
of-tax money to local groups. His electoral appeal therefore cannot be separated
from accumulated service and distributive politics.

## Interpretation

Morrow supports the hypothesis that culturally conservative differentiation
helped some rural Democrats remain locally congruent. He does not establish its
independent effect. The campaign evidence presents ideology, public-school
advocacy, constituency service, and local material benefits as a single bundle.
