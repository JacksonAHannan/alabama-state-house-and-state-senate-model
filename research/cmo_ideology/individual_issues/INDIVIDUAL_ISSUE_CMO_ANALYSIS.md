# Individual issue associations with Democratic CMO

Tested **23 issues** with at least 15 observed candidate-cycles. Each model adjusts for election cycle and chamber and uses HC3 robust uncertainty. Positive estimates mean movement toward the named positive pole is associated with higher selection-aware CMO.

**9 issues** clear a 10% Benjamini-Hochberg false-discovery-rate threshold.

The screen is observational and exploratory. Missing issue positions remain missing; they are not coded as neutral.
